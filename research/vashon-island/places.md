# Vashon Island — Locations & Landmarks

## Major Geographic Features

### Vashon Island
- **Type:** Island
- **Location:** Central Puget Sound, King County, Washington
- **Coordinates:** 47.4079 N, 122.4630 W (approximate center)
- **Description:** Approximately 13 miles long and 8 miles wide, Vashon Island is the largest island in Puget Sound south of the San Juan Islands. Accessible only by ferry or private boat. Population 11,055 (2020 census).
- **Related entries:** All

### Maury Island
- **Type:** Island (connected to Vashon via tombolo since 1913)
- **Location:** Southeast of Vashon Island
- **Description:** Smaller island connected to Vashon by a constructed tombolo at Portage. Before 1913, the two islands were separate at high tide. Home to the historic Dockton drydock.
- **Related entries:** pre-001, ind-001, exp-002, ear-002, gro-002

### Puget Sound
- **Type:** Estuary / inlet system
- **Location:** Western Washington
- **Description:** The deep saltwater inlet system that surrounds Vashon Island. Carved by glacial activity during the last ice age. Defines the island's maritime character and isolation.
- **Related entries:** pre-001, exp-001, exp-002

## Communities & Settlements

### Vashon Town
- **Type:** Unincorporated community (island center)
- **Description:** The main commercial center of the island, located roughly in the center. Home to shops, restaurants, and the primary ferry terminal access road.

### Burton
- **Type:** Historic community
- **Description:** Located on the eastern shore near the Portage area. Site of Vashon College (opened 1892). One of the earliest settlement clusters on the island.

### Dockton
- **Type:** Historic community
- **Location:** Southwestern shore of Maury Island
- **Description:** Named for its drydock facility, the only one of its kind on Puget Sound in the 1890s. Maritime industrial community that attracted workers during the growth era.
- **Related entries:** gro-002

## Historic Sites

### Mukai Farm and Garden
- **Type:** Heritage site
- **Description:** The farmstead of Denichiro Mukai, including a cold-process fruit barreling plant built in 1926. Now preserved as a historic site documenting the Japanese American agricultural legacy on the island.
- **Related entries:** ear-001

### Portage (Tombolo)
- **Type:** Land bridge / infrastructure
- **Description:** The constructed connection between Vashon and Maury Islands, built in 1913 to replace the tidal sandbar. Located at the narrow point between the two islands.
- **Related entries:** ear-002, ear-003

## Transportation Landmarks

### Fauntleroy Ferry Terminal
- **Type:** Ferry terminal (mainland side)
- **Description:** West Seattle terminal for the Washington State Ferries route to Vashon Island. Primary connection to the Seattle metropolitan area.
- **Related entries:** fer-001

### Vashon Ferry Terminal (North End)
- **Type:** Ferry terminal (island side)
- **Description:** Northern ferry terminal receiving service from Fauntleroy (West Seattle). Primary commuter route.

### Tahlequah Ferry Terminal (South End)
- **Type:** Ferry terminal (island side)
- **Description:** Southern ferry terminal connecting to Point Defiance in Tacoma. Secondary route.

## Indigenous Sites

### Five Village Sites
- **Type:** Archaeological / cultural sites
- **Description:** Five principal longhouse village sites established by the sx̌ʷəbabš (S'Homamish) people approximately 500 years ago. Specific locations are culturally sensitive.
- **Related entries:** ind-001
