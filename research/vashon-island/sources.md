# Vashon Island — Bibliography & Sources

## Source Ranking

Sources are ranked by reliability:
- **Primary:** Original documents, archives, official records
- **Secondary:** Academic publications, museum materials, curated historical databases
- **Tertiary:** Encyclopedias, general reference websites

---

## Secondary Sources

### HistoryLink.org
- **Title:** Vashon-Maury Island -- Thumbnail History
- **URL:** https://www.historylink.org/file/3909
- **Type:** Secondary
- **Description:** The Free Online Encyclopedia of Washington State History. Peer-reviewed articles on Pacific Northwest history. The Vashon-Maury Island thumbnail history provides a comprehensive overview from indigenous habitation through the modern era.
- **Used for:** Timeline across all eras; general reference

### Vashon Heritage Museum
- **Title:** Vashon History
- **URL:** https://www.vashonheritagemuseum.org/vashon-history
- **Type:** Secondary
- **Description:** The island's dedicated heritage museum. Maintains archives, photographs, and oral histories. Online materials provide overview history and links to deeper resources.
- **Used for:** Indigenous history, general timeline, cultural context

### VashonHistory.com (Bruce Haulman)
- **Title:** A Brief History of Vashon Island
- **URL:** https://www.vashonhistory.com/Publications/brief_history.htm
- **Type:** Secondary
- **Description:** Website maintained by island historian Bruce Haulman. Contains publications, timeline information, and detailed articles on specific periods and topics.
- **Used for:** Indigenous peoples, settlement history, detailed period coverage

### VashonHistory.com — Settlements Timeline
- **Title:** Settlements 1865-1890
- **URL:** https://www.vashonhistory.com/Vashon%20History/Timeline/settlement.htm
- **Type:** Secondary
- **Description:** Detailed timeline of the pioneer settlement era, including specific dates and individuals.
- **Used for:** Pioneer era details, Mathew Bridges, S.D. Sherman

### Vashon-Maury Island Community Guide
- **Title:** Delve into the Fascinating History of Vashon Island
- **URL:** https://vashon-maury.com/vashon-island-history/
- **Type:** Secondary
- **Description:** Community website with historical overview and visitor information. Covers key events and personalities.
- **Used for:** Denichiro Mukai, agricultural history
