# Vashon Island — Bibliography & Sources

## Source Ranking

Sources are ranked by reliability:
- **Primary:** Original documents, archives, official records
- **Secondary:** Academic publications, museum materials, curated historical databases
- **Tertiary:** Encyclopedias, general reference websites

## Primary Sources

### USGS Geologic Map of the Vashon Quadrangle
- **Title:** Geologic Map of the Vashon 7.5' Quadrangle -- USGS
- **URL:** https://pubs.usgs.gov/sim/3328/downloads/sim_3328_pamphlet.pdf
- **Type:** Primary
- **Description:** Official U.S. Geological Survey geologic map and pamphlet for the Vashon 7.5-minute quadrangle. Documents the glacial stratigraphy (Vashon Till, Esperance Sand, Lawton Clay) and geological formations of the island.
- **Used for:** Prehistory geology, bluff and ravine formation, glacial deposits
